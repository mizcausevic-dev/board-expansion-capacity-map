# Kinetic Gain Embedded

Board Expansion Capacity Map is designed to open an embedded executive-intelligence engagement:

- convert scattered growth signals, bottleneck pockets, and operator-capacity evidence into one board-readable capacity packet
- tighten the leadership story around what can unlock next instead of overclaiming theoretical gains
- create a reusable operating layer for committee reviews, diligence rooms, and investor updates
- prove expansion capacity without weakening the systems that still have to carry the company
