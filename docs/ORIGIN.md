# Origin

Board Expansion Capacity Map exists after the scorecards, trust-room, procurement-answer, RFP assembly, proof-gap, board memo, benchmark, scenario, allocation, priority, reallocation, preservation, takeout, efficiency-proof, leverage, and scaling-readiness layers.

The earlier tools show where to invest, what to freeze, what to cut, and what already landed. This repo turns that stack into one capacity surface so leadership can explain what can expand now, where bottlenecks remain, which owners are ready to unlock more throughput, and which growth claims actually survive a board or diligence room.
